# Pipeline lifecycle: source evidence and boundaries

This is a **source-navigation and evidence map**, not a promise about every Pipeline journey. It was inspected against Preview `873773e2d219ef0ec758361df3cec5509eaeef04`; the links below follow the revision containing this document. Recheck owners, migrations and tests when the code changes. [Product direction](product-direction.md#pipeline-revisions-voting-and-publication) and the [Confluence blueprint](https://pyralisholdings.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PYPH/pages/917505) describe intended experience; source and test definitions alone do not establish a deployed database, live settings or a signed-in hosted journey.

## Submit, visibility and authority

- [Generic upload submission](../src/features/pipeline/lib/pipelineUploadSubmission.ts) and the [submission route](../src/app/api/pipeline/%5BsubmissionId%5D/route.ts) have their own validation and authorization. Submitting work, voting, publishing and using an acquired resource are distinct operations.
- The [vote route](../src/app/api/pipeline/%5BsubmissionId%5D/vote/route.ts) derives the actor from the authenticated session, requires `assets.review`, checks the selected revision's visibility and self-voting policy, and rate-limits attempts. [Contributor access](../src/features/contributor-access/server/access.ts) distinguishes Owner and scoped Contributor access; account visibility is not itself permission to perform every action.
- [Revision visibility](../src/features/pipeline/lib/pipelineVisibility.ts) keeps another Contributor's draft private and hides purging revisions; eligible previously shared Trash has different visibility from a private draft. The [voteability check](../src/features/pipeline/lib/pipelineLibrary.ts) rejects drafts and nonshared Trash. Scope, visibility, moderation and destructive-claim checks still apply; “shared Trash” does not mean publicly visible to every account.

## Votes, policy and publication

The [native vote and rebalance migration](../supabase/migrations/20261101010617_pipeline_vote_trash_recovery.sql) records one positive or negative vote per actor and exact submission; an opposite vote replaces it. A positive preference moves off sibling revisions in the same lineage; a person can retain negative votes on multiple eligible revisions. A vote carries weight one. The route reads self-voting policy; the checked-in [self-voting migration](../supabase/migrations/20260924064127_enable_revision_self_voting.sql) requires the stored setting to be true and the settings update function writes true. Do not infer that an Owner can disable it just because a policy field exists in application types.

The [application defaults](../src/features/pipeline/lib/pipelineItems.ts) specify five votes, 60% positive for the free threshold, 80% for paid, a seven-day review age, self-voting enabled and Owner vote weight one. These are checked-in defaults, **not verified live Owner settings**. The SQL decision also considers the per-type tier capacity, ranking, review age and Owner overrides. A vote calls rebalance; it is not a guarantee that an item is published.

At this source baseline, eligible ordinary new assets can be automatically published after the applicable conditions and capacity checks. Initial Templates and revisions in an already-live lineage instead enter the Owner-candidate path. That candidate branch occurs before the minimum-vote branch: a `publish_candidate` status alone does not prove the vote minimum or age was met. Specialized [media and Template/style review publication checks](../supabase/migrations/20261027023820_pipeline_style_review_candidates.sql) additionally enforce editorial, base-version, payload and conflict conditions where applicable. Do not generalize one lane's checks to every asset type.

## Trash, current publication and recovery

The same native rebalance includes active review items, eligible current publications and unheld vote-origin Trash. An eligible current publication below the free threshold after the minimum votes and review age can enter vote-origin Trash when native retained-consumer admission permits it. The initial-Template candidate branch precedes the below-threshold archive branch, and Owner status overrides affect the effective result, so meeting the threshold and admission checks does not guarantee removal. On the vote-origin removal path, the registry row is archived and hidden while its exact pointer and source bytes are retained; a retained pointer is therefore not proof the resource remains publicly discoverable. If admission blocks removal, the current publication remains protected.

Eligible vote-origin Trash can re-enter policy after changed votes; an unchanged decision fingerprint prevents immediate repeat Trash after restoration. Manual Contributor or Owner Trash, retention holds and destructive claims are separate authority boundaries. An original Contributor's revision recovery and Owner-only catalog Trash use different [revision recovery](../src/features/pipeline/server/revisionRecovery.ts) and [catalog Trash](../src/features/pipeline/server/catalogTrash.ts) commands. Recovery does not grant permission to replace a newer live revision. A retention date does not itself authorize purging previously published material, and an uncertain destructive attempt requires reconciliation rather than a blind retry.

The source-defined [vote/Trash recovery SQL fixture](../supabase/tests/pipeline_vote_trash_recovery.sql) includes current-publication removal and newer-pointer recovery cases. It is a test definition, not a claim that it ran against this document's target database.

## Exact source is not general catalog access

The [exact published source reader](../src/features/pipeline/server/exactPublishedSource.ts) checks the supplied submission, lineage and registry identity. Its initial authorization admits the Owner, original Contributor, or an account whose tier can see that exact current published revision; tier visibility alone does not authorize a historical revision. It rejects mismatched identities, lineage catalog Trash or claim, lineage or submission purge state, submission Trash, and never-published source; a `current` selection must still match the live pointer. The reader does not itself select every revision-level claim field. Pending Templates use a different Owner candidate reader. A visible review card, historical title, or newer same-name revision is not a substitute for authorization to read the selected exact source.
